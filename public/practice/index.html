Digital citizenship · Grades 6–8 · 15–20 min

Glimmer

A made-up social app. Your account is "private." So what can a stranger actually see?

  • Guess what a stranger sees, then check with "View as a stranger"
  • Find and change the privacy settings yourself
  • Decide on two friend requests, one of them fake
  • Fix a post's audience and location before it goes out

No sign-in. Everyone in Glimmer is made up, and nothing students do in it leaves their device.

Open Glimmer
PredictEvery task starts with a question. Students commit to an answer before the app shows them.
Find it yourselfNothing is labeled "click here." Students navigate the app the way they would a real one.
Hint if stuckThe first hint is a nudge in words. The second puts a ring on the exact spot.

For teachers: Glimmer is not a copy of any real company's app. Its settings work the way most social apps' do, so what students practice carries over to Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat and whatever comes next. The panel's "Start over" resets the app for the next student.
