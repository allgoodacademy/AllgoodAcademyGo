For teachers — curriculum framing Tap to open

Key concept: Perspective  ·  Related concepts: Credibility, Choice, Causality, Bias  ·  Global context: Identities and relationships  ·  Year group: MYP 2–4

Statement of inquiry: Urgency, audience and repetition narrow the choices available to us; recognising these forces helps us make more deliberate decisions about what we believe and how we act.

The through-line: four misconceptions, one mechanism underneath all of them — the student mistakes intensity for effectiveness. More voices, more certainty, more audience, more pressure. In every case the thing that feels like it is making them stronger is the thing removing their options.
