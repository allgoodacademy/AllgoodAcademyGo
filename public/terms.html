3. Eligibility and accounts

Age requirement

You must be at least 13 years old to create an account or sign in with Google. Learners under 13 use the Recruit Code system — an automatically generated three-word passphrase with an auto-assigned display name and avatar. No account is created, and no name or email address is ever requested.

Guest sessions

You can start any lesson without signing in. A guest session is given a readable session name generated on your own device. Guest progress is kept only for that session unless you choose to save it.

Recruit Codes are not passwords

A Recruit Code is deliberately memorable so that a learner without an email address can resume their work on another device. Anyone who knows the exact passphrase can resume that progress. Please treat it accordingly, and contact us if a code needs to be retired.

Account security

If you hold a 13+ account, you are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your login credentials and for activity that occurs under your account. Tell us promptly if you believe an account has been accessed by someone else.
