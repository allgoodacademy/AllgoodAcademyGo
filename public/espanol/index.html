Viaja en español.

Mini interactive stories inspired by real travel. Get lost, order dinner, survive an interview, all in Spanish. In every story, the Spanish you choose changes what happens next.

Elige tu historia ↓For teachers

Hola, soy William.

I taught Spanish for six years. I also love to travel, and some of my favorite memories from trips are small moments when I had to figure out what to say, like asking for help at a counter or ordering dinner with people I'd just met.

I've used those experiences to create these mini stories. Each one is short and set in a real place, and the choices you make in Spanish change how it plays out.

¡Buen viaje!

— William Allgood, founder of Allgood Academy
