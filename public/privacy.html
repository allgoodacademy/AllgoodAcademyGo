Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

This policy explains what Allgood Academy collects, why, and what we will never do with it. It covers AllgoodOS™ and our GoodBlocks™ learning modules, and it applies to learners, teachers and school staff alike.

Request a review or deletion

The short version

The full policy is below. This is the honest summary of it.

  • We never sell your data, and we never use it for advertising. There is no advertising anywhere in Allgood Academy.
  • Learners under 13 never give us a name or an email address. They get an auto-generated passphrase and an auto-assigned avatar instead. We hold no real-world identifying detail for them.
  • Teachers sign in with a real Google account — because they're adults setting up their own classroom.
  • A learner can type free text in an end-of-lesson comment box. That's the one place something identifying could be entered, and we tell you exactly what happens to it in Section 3.
  • You can ask us to review or delete a specific learner's data at any time, and we'll do it.

1. Who this policy covers

Three different people use Allgood Academy, and we collect very different things from each. Most of the confusion about student privacy comes from policies that blur these together, so we've kept them separate throughout.

  • Learner Recruits (under 13). No account, no name, no email. Identified only by a Recruit Code.
  • Learners aged 13 and over. May sign in with Google, which shares a name, email address and profile picture with us.
  • Teachers and school staff. Sign in with a real Google account to create and manage a Task Force (a classroom).

You can also use most of Allgood Academy without signing in at all. A visitor who starts a lesson is given a readable session name generated on their own device — nothing is written to our database, and nothing is reserved, unless and until they choose to save their progress.

2. What we collect

From Learner Recruits (under 13)

A Recruit Code — a memorable three-word passphrase, for example "arctic-fox-trot" — generated automatically. A display name and an avatar, both assigned automatically. Nothing a learner types identifies them, because they are never asked to type anything identifying.

Alongside that pseudonymous identity we store what the learner did: answers and choices made inside GoodBlocks™, scores, completion status, which Case they reached, time spent on a module, and which classroom code (if any) they joined.

From learners aged 13 and over

If a learner signs in with Google, we receive their name, email address and profile picture from Google, and use them to create their Digital Passport and issue certificates and badges. We store the same learning and progress data described above.

From teachers and school staff

Name and email address from the Google account used to sign in, the classrooms created, and the roster of learners who joined them. Before creating a classroom for the first time, a teacher is shown a plain-language notice and must confirm they are authorized staff using this tool under their school's policies.

3. Free text, and the one place it exists

At the end of a GoodBlock™ a learner may leave an optional star rating and a written comment. That comment box is the only place in Allgood Academy where a learner types words that we store. We can't inspect what someone chooses to write there, so we are explicit about how we handle it:

  • The comment is stored with the learner's pseudonymous identity and an age-tier marker, so that a request to delete a specific child's comment can actually be honored.
  • It can be read only by a small number of named Allgood Academy administrators. It is never shown to other learners, and it is not shown on a teacher's roster.
  • It is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared outside Allgood Academy.

We ask learners not to include their real name or any personal detail in that box. If you believe a child has entered something identifying, email us and we will delete it — see Section 9.

4. What is stored on the device

To keep a session working between page loads and visits, we cache a small amount of non-identifying data in the browser's Local Storage on the device itself. This includes a session identifier, the auto-assigned display name and avatar, an age-tier marker, whether the session is a guest session, and flags recording which modules have been completed.

This data stays on the device. Clearing your browser's site data removes it. On a shared device — a school Chromebook, for example — signing out or clearing site data is what prevents the next person from resuming the previous session.

5. How we use it

  • To run the learning experience. Showing a learner their own progress, restoring it on a return visit, and generating certificates and badges.
  • To give teachers visibility into their own classroom. A teacher can see progress for the learners in a Task Force they own, and only that Task Force.
  • To improve the lessons. We look at aggregated performance — for example, that most learners missed a particular question — to fix content that isn't teaching well.
  • To keep accounts working and secure. Maintaining sessions and authenticating sign-in.

6. What we never do

  • We do not sell personal data to anyone, under any circumstances.
  • We do not use learner data for advertising, and we do not show advertising in Allgood Academy.
  • We do not build behavioral or marketing profiles of learners.
  • We do not require a learner under 13 to provide a name, an email address, or any other real-world identifying detail in order to learn.

Allgood Academy is free for learners, permanently. That is a product commitment, and it is part of why none of the above is ever on the table.

7. Age restrictions (COPPA)

You must be 13 years of age or older to create an account or sign in via Google.

If you are under 13, you use the Recruit Code system instead of creating an account. A Recruit Code is a 3-word passphrase (for example, "arctic-fox-trot") automatically generated for you — no real name, email address, or account creation is ever required. Your display name and avatar are also generated automatically, so nothing you enter identifies you personally. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information (PII) from children under 13. If we discover that a user under 13 has created a real (13+) account, we will delete that account and all associated data.

A Recruit Code is a convenience, not a password. Anyone who knows the exact passphrase can resume that learner's progress — that is deliberately how a child moves to a different device without an email address. Treat it the way you would a library card number, not a password, and tell us if one needs to be retired.

8. Data retention & minimization (under 13)

We collect zero Personally Identifiable Information (PII) for under-13 agent learners. Learners under 13 never provide a name, email address, or any other real-world identifying detail — instead, each is assigned a pseudonymized Recruit Code (a memorable three-word passphrase) and an auto-assigned avatar, which together serve as that learner's identity within AllgoodOS™ and let them resume progress on a different device.

All anonymous interaction data and telemetry logs are permanently purged from our systems after 90 days of inactivity. This includes session records (time on task, steps reached, completion status) and event logs (module opens, in-module choices, module completions) tied to an under-13 learner's pseudonymized session. Records still receiving activity are retained only for as long as that activity continues; once 90 days pass with no further activity, the underlying data is deleted from our production database.

9. Requesting a review or deletion

If you are a parent, guardian, teacher or school administrator and you want to review or delete a specific learner's data, email us and we will take care of it. You do not need an account to make this request, and you do not need to explain why.

Tell us what you can — a Recruit Code, a display name, a classroom code, or the approximate dates — and we will locate the records and confirm in writing once they are removed.

10. Third-party services

We keep this list short on purpose, and we name everything on it.

  • Google Firebase — authentication, database and hosting. Learner and teacher records described in this policy are stored here.
  • Google Analytics — used on our public marketing pages (the homepage, For Teachers, and About) to understand how people find us, and on the signed-in dashboard only for accounts confirmed to be 13 or over. It does not load on any surface reachable by an under-13 session, and it never loads unconditionally.

Our GoodBlocks™ and Challenges load their code and styling from our own servers rather than from third-party content networks, so a learner working through a lesson is not making requests to other companies.

11. Schools, districts and teachers

When a teacher creates a Task Force and shares a classroom code, learners who join it appear on that teacher's roster under their pseudonymous display names. A teacher sees only the Task Force they own.

We do not ask a school to provide consent on parents' behalf, and we do not rely on a school having done so, because we do not collect personally identifiable information from learners under 13 in the first place. A teacher or staff member using Allgood Academy with students remains responsible for doing so in line with their school's and district's own policies.

Schools and districts that need a written agreement, a data-processing addendum, or answers for a vendor review should email us — we would rather answer a procurement questionnaire than have you guess from this page.

12. Data security

Access to learner records is restricted at the database level: a teacher can read only their own classroom's roster, administrative access is limited to a named allowlist, and a learner can read only their own progress. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we update the "Last updated" date at the top. That date reflects the last time the words on this page actually changed — it is not generated automatically, so you can use it to tell whether anything is different since you last read it.

If we make a change that materially affects what we collect from learners or how long we keep it, we will say so plainly at the top of this page rather than relying on you to notice.

14. Contact us

Questions about this policy, a deletion request, or a district vendor review all go to the same place: learning@allgoodacademy.com. A real person reads it.
