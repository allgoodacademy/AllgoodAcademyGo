For teachers

The science comes from NASA, NOAA, the National Weather Service and the U.S. Naval Observatory. Sun height, daylight and sunlight energy are calculated from real solar geometry for each city's latitude. The model leaves out air, clouds, oceans and weather. Nothing students type is stored.
