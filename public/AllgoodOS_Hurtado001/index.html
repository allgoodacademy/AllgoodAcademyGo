Compare loan types

Same price, rate and term for each. Tap one to use it in the numbers above. Your lender sets the real rate and rules for each program.

  • FHA: as little as 3.5% down. Mortgage insurance is 1.75% upfront (added to the loan) plus 0.55% a year, and it stays for the life of the loan with under 10% down. FHA caps the loan amount by county.
  • VA: for eligible veterans, service members and some surviving spouses, with a Certificate of Eligibility. No monthly mortgage insurance. A one-time funding fee (2.15% for first use with under 5% down, less with more down) is added to the loan, and is waived for veterans with a service-connected disability rating.
  • Conventional: mortgage insurance applies under 20% down and can come off later.
