How to read these numbers

I built this sheet so you can see real figures before you commit to anything. Here's exactly what they are, and what they aren't.

  • My best estimate as of November 4, 2026. Prices and options come from the builder and Stellar MLS. That information is deemed reliable but not guaranteed.
  • Subject to change without notice. Prices, options, incentives, homesites, interest rates and availability can change at any time.
  • The contract is final. Your price and plan are set only in the builder's purchase agreement. A price range means the cost depends on your other choices.
  • Not a loan offer. The rate and down payment are examples you can change. Payments are illustrations, not an offer to lend and not an Annual Percentage Rate (APR). Your lender's Loan Estimate shows your real terms.
  • Taxes, insurance and HOA are estimates. I'll confirm each one for the homesite you choose.
  • Photos show the model home. It's decorated and may include furnishings and features that aren't in the price. Sample Realty is not the builder.
