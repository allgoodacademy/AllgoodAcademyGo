How I build Allgood Academy

One founder. A full-stack team.

I started as a classroom teacher. Today I lead instructional design at a national education nonprofit. In the classroom, I leaned on technology to give my students better lessons than I could have made on my own. I use AI the same way now.

Allgood Academy is just me, but I think of my AI tools as my full-stack team. I set the vision and make the calls. They help me build. And nothing reaches a student until I've checked it myself.

What AI never does here

  • No AI chatting with your students. Every line Jodi says is written and checked ahead of time.
  • No student information goes into AI tools. Not names, not answers, not progress.
  • No AI grading. Jodi doesn't grade at all, and neither does a machine.
  • No fake people. No AI-generated people in our photos or ads.
  • No copied voices. Every AI voice belongs to an invented character.
  • Nothing ships unchecked. If I haven't played it, read it and heard it, it isn't live.
