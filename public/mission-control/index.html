Before you set up Mission Control

What we collect from your students: a nickname they choose (never a real name or email), their scores, and their activity in the lessons you assign. That's it.

What you see: teachers see the steps and choices of students in their own class, under the nickname the student picked.

Why: so you can see their progress and so we can run the lessons themselves.

Retention: kept while the account is active; you or a parent can request deletion anytime.

Data is never sold, and never used for advertising.

Setting up gives you a class code to share with students, and a view of their progress.
