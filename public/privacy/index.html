Privacy at Allgood Academy

Last updated:

What we collect from students

Students sign in anonymously — we never ask a student for their real name or email address. A student picks (or is given) a nickname that isn't tied to their real identity. What we do store, tied to that anonymous account, is:

  • A nickname (not a real name)
  • Scores and completion status for the lessons a student takes
  • Which classroom code (if any) a student joined
  • Basic activity timestamps (last login, when a module was completed)

What we collect from teachers

A teacher or staff member signs in with their real Google account (name and email), since they're an adult setting up their own classroom. Before creating a classroom for the first time, a teacher is shown a plain-language notice and must confirm they're authorized staff using the tool under their school's policies.

Why we collect it

Solely to run the learning experience itself: showing a student their own progress, letting a teacher see how their class is doing, and letting us fix bugs or improve lessons. We do not sell data, and we do not use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Account and progress data is kept for as long as the account is in use, so a student's progress isn't lost between sessions. A teacher or parent can request deletion at any time — see below.
