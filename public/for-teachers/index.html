First: what's a GoodBlock?

A GoodBlock is one lab. One complete, standalone interactive lesson a student finishes in a sitting — built on our own lesson engine rather than assembled from slides and a quiz.

Your students will see the word Lab in the interface, because that's the plain word for the thing they're doing. GoodBlock™ is what we call the format itself. Same object, two audiences. Nothing is a "course."

CASE

A Case

One interactive moment inside a lesson. A situation, a real choice, and Jodi explaining the mechanism behind whatever the student picked.

GOODBLOCK

A GoodBlock

Five to seven Cases that build one idea. This is the unit you assign. Roughly twenty minutes. Your students call it a Lab.

PACK

A Lab Pack

Several GoodBlocks on a shared theme, closing with a scored Challenge that pulls from all of them. This is the unit, if you want a unit.

What we don't collect.

This is the section a parent will ask you about. Short version: there is nothing here you'd have to defend.

No email from anyone under 13

Younger students get a recruit code instead of an account. It's the only thing tying their progress to them, and they write it down themselves. We never ask for a name, an email, or a birthday.

No advertising, ever

There are no ads in any GoodBlock, no third-party ad tracking, and no arrangement where anyone pays to appear in front of your students. There's no version of this product where that changes.

Student data isn't sold or shared

Completion records exist so you can see who finished. We also record how the lesson itself went — which case a student reached, how long they were active, which options they picked — because that's how we find out which lessons work and fix the ones that don't. All of it is keyed to the same anonymous ID as their progress, never to a name or an email, and for under-13 Recruits there is no name or email on file at all. None of it is sold, licensed, or handed to a partner.

Free means free

Not a trial, not a freemium tier that locks the good GoodBlocks. Students never pay for their own learning. If revenue comes, it comes from optional educator and administrator tools.

Questions teachers actually ask.

Do my students need accounts?

No. A student can open a GoodBlock and work through the whole thing without signing in. We ask about saving progress at the end, once there's something worth saving. For completions to reach your Task Force, a student has to save their progress and then join with your code from the dashboard — there's no way to do that at the start of a lesson yet, so a class that never saves won't show up.

What age is this for?

Built for middle school, roughly grades 6 through 9. Younger students can do it with support, and high school teachers use the Real World Ready GoodBlocks. The content assumes a student who already has a phone and a group chat.

Will it work on our school network?

It runs in a browser with nothing to install. If your district filters aggressively, allgoodacademy.com on its own isn't enough — the lessons also load from gstatic.com and googleapis.com (sign-in and saved progress) and from cdn.tailwindcss.com, unpkg.com and cdnjs.cloudflare.com (layout and icons). We're moving those last three in-house so the list gets shorter. If something's blocked, tell us what and we'll work around it — that feedback is genuinely useful to us.

How long does one take?

About twenty minutes for most students. They can stop partway and pick it up later if they're signed in. There's no timer and nothing is penalized for taking longer.

What happens if a student picks the "wrong" answer?

Nothing is marked wrong. Every choice gets an explanation of what that choice actually sets in motion. Students aren't blocked from continuing, and there's no score on the GoodBlocks themselves. The Challenges are scored, because a capstone assessment is a different thing on purpose.

Can I preview one before assigning it?

Yes, and please do. Open any GoodBlock from the list above and go through it as a student would. There's no separate teacher preview mode — you'll see exactly what they see, which is arguably the more useful preview anyway.

Do you have standards documentation for my department?

Yes. Every GoodBlock is designed around a CASEL competency, and the Digital Decisions pack maps to ISTE Standard 2. Email learning@allgoodacademy.com and we'll send the documentation over.

Is it really free, or free for now?

Really free, permanently, for students. That's a product principle, not a launch promotion. If we ever charge, it'll be schools paying for administrator and educator tooling — never a student paying for access to their own learning.
